# Home (site-wide world)

Scope: home page, and the visual world every route inherits (resources, guide page, about, join, review form, 404). Visitor mode: Persuade on home; the library routes lean Operate/Read inside the same world.

Audience and job: students who need a guide (find it, open it) and students who might join (see how, act). Action: search the library or open a volume; secondary action: write the next volume (join).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Math Mentors is a numbered paperback series: every guide is a volume with its own black-and-white geometric cover drawn from its real topic. Refuses the editorial hairline-and-kicker page and the rounded-card edtech landing.

OWN-WORLD: Paper #fafafa ground, ink #171717 solid fields, gray #737373, rule #e5e5e5; no other hue. Schibsted Grotesk only, 900 for titles set tight, 400-500 for text, tabular figures for volume numbers (No. 001). Components are book parts: covers, spines, credits, colophon, index, back cover. Square corners, 2px ink rules, whole-cover modules.

STORY: The visitor sees a real series, opens a volume or searches the index, learns it is free and peer-written, and is invited to write the next numbered volume.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, the masthead line in 900 at display scale with a working "find a guide" search and subject index under it. Right, the newest volume as a large cover with its spine, beside a blank dashed next volume that links to Join.

FORM: Mid-century mathematics paperback series, position 5 of 7, seed key f4240109. Raises: HyperCard (blank next volume is the join door), Ikebana (one off-center figure per cover), Azulejo (whole-module grid), Star atlas (topic index into volumes), Nixie (fixed-place numbers). Signature interaction: shelf spines that pull up on hover/focus to reveal their cover.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
